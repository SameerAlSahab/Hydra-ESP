# Code of Conduct

## Our Expectations

Hydra-ESP is a community for security research, development, and learning. Everyone should be able to take part without dealing with harassment, personal attacks, or unnecessary hostility.

Treat people with respect, regardless of their background, identity, beliefs, education, or level of technical experience. You don't have to agree with everyone, but you should be able to disagree without making things personal.

## Acceptable Behavior

* Be respectful when discussing ideas, reviewing code, or giving feedback.
* Keep criticism focused on the work, not the person behind it.
* Take responsibility for your mistakes and correct them when possible.
* Respect other people's privacy and boundaries.
* Keep discussions relevant and useful to the project.
* Help maintain a space where people can learn, contribute, and ask questions without being mocked.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable in Hydra-ESP community spaces:

* Harassment, threats, or unwanted sexual attention.
* Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate attempts to provoke others.
* Discrimination or derogatory remarks targeting individuals or groups.
* Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
* Repeatedly contacting or targeting someone after being asked to stop.
* Using Hydra-ESP code, tools, or community spaces to coordinate unauthorized attacks against real systems or people.
* Any other behavior that makes participation hostile or unsafe for others.

## Security Research and Responsible Use

Hydra-ESP is a security research and educational project. Discussions about vulnerabilities, attack techniques, exploit development, and defensive measures are part of the project.

However, there is a clear distinction between researching security and targeting systems without permission. Do not use Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, or other project channels to seek help attacking systems you do not own or have explicit authorization to test.

Threads that cross this line may be closed or locked by the maintainers.

## Where These Rules Apply

This Code of Conduct applies to all official Hydra-ESP spaces, including Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, and other project channels.

It also applies when someone is representing Hydra-ESP in public or at an event.

## Reporting a Violation

If you experience or witness a violation, contact the project maintainer, Sameer Al Sahab.

You can report an incident privately through a GitHub Security Advisory or by email using the address listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as privately as reasonably possible. Anyone handling a report must respect the privacy of the people involved.

## Enforcement

The response depends on the severity of the incident and whether the behavior continues. Possible actions include:

**1. Correction**

For minor incidents, the maintainer may issue a private warning explaining what needs to change.

**2. Formal Warning**

Repeated or more serious behavior may result in a formal warning and restrictions on contact with the people involved. Further violations may lead to a ban.

**3. Temporary Ban**

Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary ban from participating in the community.

**4. Permanent Ban**

Harassment, threats, repeated violations, or other severe misconduct may result in a permanent ban.

The maintainer may remove or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate these rules. Where appropriate, the reason for the decision will be explained.

These measures are intended to keep the project productive and safe, not to prevent legitimate technical criticism or good-faith disagreement.
